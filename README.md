## About This Repository

Welcome! This repository documents my journey into **cybersecurity, ethical hacking, and penetration testing**.

I'm building my foundations in networking, Linux, security concepts, and programming while practicing in controlled lab environments. Here, I document what I learn, the tools I use, the challenges I encounter, and the reasoning behind my findings.

My goal is not just to learn how to use security tools, but to understand **how they work, why they work, and what their results actually mean**.

## Learning Objectives

- Understand networking fundamentals, TCP/IP, ports, and protocols.
- Develop practical Linux and Windows administration skills.
- Learn reconnaissance, enumeration, and vulnerability assessment.
- Understand common web application vulnerabilities and their mitigations.
- Improve my Python programming and scripting skills.
- Practice interpreting network traffic and analyzing technical evidence.
- Develop a structured methodology for authorized penetration testing.

| Topic | Areas of Study |

## Lab Reports

My reports focus on concepts, methodology, evidence, troubleshooting, and lessons learned rather than simply listing commands.

Each report may include:

- **Objective:** What I wanted to investigate.
- **Key concepts:** The underlying technical principles.
- **Methodology:** The steps taken during the investigation.
- **Evidence and findings:** What the tools revealed and how I interpreted the results.
- **Troubleshooting:** Problems encountered and how I investigated them.
- **Security analysis:** Potential impact and recommended mitigations.
- **Lessons learned:** What I understood better and what I still need to study.
